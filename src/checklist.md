### p1-1.3.1 | 1.3.1 | Info and Relationships (Automated Syntax) | A

### p1-2.4.2 | 2.4.2 | Page Titled (Presence Pre-check) | A

### p1-3.1.1 | 3.1.1 | Language of Page | A
- Section 508: E205.4
- Web: Verify <html> element has a valid lang attribute (e.g., lang='en').

### p1-4.1.2 | 4.1.2 | Name, Role, Value (Syntax Pre-check) | A

## ANDI Visual & DOM Inspection

Tools: ANDI Bookmarklet, Colour Contrast Analyser (CCA)

### p2-1.1.1 | 1.1.1 | Non-text Content (Quality & Decorative) | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 7.A / 7.B / 7.C)
- ANDI module: Graphics/Images
- Web: In ANDI Graphics/Images: verify informative images/SVGs have concise, accurate alt text; decorative images use alt='' or aria-hidden='true'; complex charts have text alternatives.
- PDF: In Tags pane: verify <Figure> tags have meaningful Alt Text, decorative graphics are marked as Artifacts, and drop caps use ActualText.
- Hint: Logos/images of text must include the exact visible text in their alt attribute.

### p2-1.3.1 | 1.3.1 | Info and Relationships (Headings, Lists, Tables, Fieldsets) | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 10.B / 12.A / 13.A)
- ANDI module: Structures & Tables
- Web: In ANDI Structures & Tables: verify headings (<h1>-<h6>) match visual hierarchy without skipped levels; lists use <ul>/<ol>/<dl>; data tables use <th scope='col|row'>; radio/checkbox groups use <fieldset>/<legend>.
- PDF: Verify <H1>-<H6> hierarchy; verify <L>/<LI>/<Lbl>/<LBody> list nesting; verify <TH> has Scope set to Row/Column in Tag Properties.
- Hint: Use only one <h1> per page for main page purpose; do not use heading tags purely for font sizing.

### p2-1.3.2 | 1.3.2 | Meaningful Sequence | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 13.B)
- ANDI module: Structures
- Web: In ANDI Structures: toggle Reading Order / linearize page to verify DOM sequence matches logical visual reading order.
- PDF: Compare Tags pane order and Reading Order panel against visual layout top-to-bottom, left-to-right.
- Hint: Watch out for CSS Flexbox/Grid 'order' or absolute positioning that scrambles DOM reading order.

### p2-1.3.3 | 1.3.3 | Sensory Characteristics | A
- Section 508: E205.4
- ANDI module: Structures
- Web: Review instructional text to ensure cues do not rely solely on shape, size, visual location, orientation, or sound (e.g., 'click the round green button on the right').
- PDF: Ensure document instructions reference explicit section headings or labels rather than visual position alone.

### p2-1.4.1 | 1.4.1 | Use of Color | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 11.B)
- ANDI module: Color Contrast
- Web: Verify color is not the sole indicator of meaning: inline links must have underlines (or >=3:1 contrast with surrounding text + non-color hover/focus cue); error/success states and chart series need icons, text, or patterns.
- PDF: Verify inline links, form error states, and chart legends use underlines, labels, or patterns in addition to color.

### p2-1.4.3 | 1.4.3 | Contrast (Minimum) | AA
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 11.A)
- ANDI module: Color Contrast
- Web: In ANDI Color Contrast: verify normal text >= 4.5:1 and large text (>=18pt or >=14pt bold) >= 3:1. Use CCA to sample text over gradients/images and custom ::selection highlight colors.
- PDF: Use Colour Contrast Analyser (CCA) to test body text (>=4.5:1), large headings (>=3:1), and text inside embedded graphics.
- Hint: ANDI flags text over background images/gradients for manual sampling with an eyedropper tool.

### p2-1.4.5 | 1.4.5 | Images of Text (Visual Verification) | AA
- Section 508: E205.4
- ANDI module: Graphics/Images
- Web: In ANDI Graphics/Images: verify no images of text are used where real CSS-styled text could achieve the same effect (brand logos exempt).
- PDF: Verify no flattened screenshots of text blocks or tables exist inside the PDF.

### p2-1.4.11 | 1.4.11 | Non-text Contrast | AA
- Section 508: N/A (WCAG 2.1+)
- ANDI module: Color Contrast
- Web: Verify UI component boundaries (input borders, buttons, checkboxes), focus rings, and meaningful chart graphics have >= 3:1 contrast against adjacent colors.
- PDF: Use Colour Contrast Analyser (CCA) on form field borders, chart bars/lines, and meaningful icons.
- Hint: Inactive/disabled controls and pure decoration are exempt.

### p2-2.4.2 | 2.4.2 | Page Titled (Descriptiveness) | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 10.A)
- ANDI module: Structures
- Web: In ANDI Structures: confirm <title> is unique and clearly describes the specific page topic or purpose.
- PDF: Verify Document Properties > Title is descriptive (not a raw filename like 'doc_final_v2.pdf').

### p2-2.4.4 | 2.4.4 | Link Purpose (In Context) | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 6.A)
- ANDI module: Links/Buttons
- Web: In ANDI Links/Buttons: inspect ANDI Output for every link; ambiguous text ('Read more', 'Click here') must have programmatic context (aria-label or enclosing sentence/heading).
- PDF: Verify <Link> tags contain descriptive text or meaningful Alt Text on the <Link> tag.
- Hint: Avoid relying on title attribute tooltips for accessible link descriptions.

### p2-2.4.6 | 2.4.6 | Headings and Labels | AA
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 10.B / 5.A)
- ANDI module: Structures & Focusable Elements
- Web: In ANDI Structures & Focusable Elements: confirm headings and form labels clearly describe their topic or purpose.
- PDF: Verify heading tags (<H1>-<H6>) and form field tooltips are clear and descriptive.

### p2-2.5.3 | 2.5.3 | Label in Name | A
- Section 508: N/A (WCAG 2.1+)
- ANDI module: Focusable Elements
- Web: In ANDI Focusable Elements: verify ANDI Output (accessible name) contains the exact visible text string shown on buttons, links, and inputs.
- PDF: Verify form field Tooltip includes the exact visible field label text.
- Hint: Critical for voice-control users (e.g., Dragon NaturallySpeaking) who speak visible button labels.

### p2-3.3.2 | 3.3.2 | Labels or Instructions | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 5.A / 5.B)
- ANDI module: Focusable Elements
- Web: In ANDI Focusable Elements: verify every input has a persistent visible label and format/required instructions associated via aria-describedby.
- PDF: Verify visible labels and format instructions (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY) appear outside placeholder text.
- Hint: Placeholders alone disappear on typing and fail 3.3.2.

### p2-4.1.2 | 4.1.2 | Name, Role, Value (Custom Controls & iframes) | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 5.C / 14.A)
- ANDI module: Focusable Elements & iframes
- Web: In ANDI Focusable Elements & iframes: verify custom controls expose accurate roles/names and every visible <iframe> has a descriptive title attribute.
- PDF: Verify interactive form fields use accurate field types (checkbox, radio, text) and descriptive tooltips.
- Hint: Use native <button> and <a href='...'> instead of <div onclick>.

### p3-2.4.1 | 2.4.1 | Bypass Blocks (Skip Link) | A

### p3-2.4.3 | 2.4.3 | Focus Order | A

### p3-2.4.5 | 2.4.5 | Multiple Ways | AA

### p3-2.4.7 | 2.4.7 | Focus Visible | AA
- Section 508: E205.4
- Web: Confirm every focusable control displays a clear, visible focus indicator when tabbed to.

### p3-2.4.11 | 2.4.11 | Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) | AA

### p3-2.5.1 | 2.5.1 | Pointer Gestures | A

### p3-2.5.2 | 2.5.2 | Pointer Cancellation | A

- PDF: N/A.

### p3-2.5.4 | 2.5.4 | Motion Actuation | A

### p3-2.5.7 | 2.5.7 | Dragging Movements | AA

### p3-2.5.8 | 2.5.8 | Target Size (Minimum) | AA

### p3-3.2.1 | 3.2.1 | On Focus | A
- Section 508: E205.4

### p3-3.2.2 | 3.2.2 | On Input | A

## Screen Reader & Multimedia Verification

Tools: VoiceOver (macOS), NVDA (Windows)

### p4-1.2.1 | 1.2.1 | Audio-only and Video-only (Prerecorded) | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 2.A / 2.B)
- Web: Verify audio-only content (podcasts) has a full text transcript; video-only animations have a text transcript or audio track.
- PDF: Embedded audio/video clips include text transcripts.

### p4-1.2.2 | 1.2.2 | Captions (Prerecorded) | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 2.A)
- Web: Play prerecorded videos with audio; confirm synchronized, accurate captions exist (including speaker IDs and sound effects).
- PDF: Embedded video includes synchronized captions.

### p4-1.2.3 | 1.2.3 | Audio Description or Media Alternative (Prerecorded) | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 2.B)
- Web: Verify prerecorded video provides either an audio description track OR a full descriptive text transcript.
- PDF: Embedded video has audio description or text transcript.

### p4-1.2.4 | 1.2.4 | Captions (Live) | AA
- Section 508: E205.4
- Web: If live streaming audio/video exists, verify real-time synchronized captions are provided.
- PDF: N/A.

### p4-1.2.5 | 1.2.5 | Audio Description (Prerecorded) | AA
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 2.B)
- Web: Confirm prerecorded video has a dedicated audio description track narrating important visual-only actions/text.
- PDF: Embedded video includes an audio description track.

### p4-1.4.2 | 1.4.2 | Audio Control | A
- Section 508: E205.4
- Web: If any audio auto-plays for >3 seconds, verify a control exists to pause/stop or adjust volume independently of system volume.
- PDF: Embedded audio does not auto-play without pause/volume control.

### p4-2.2.1 | 2.2.1 | Timing Adjustable | A
- Section 508: E205.4
- Web: If session timeouts or timed assessments exist, verify users can turn off, adjust, or extend the time limit (at least 10x with 20s warning).
- PDF: N/A (Unless timed JavaScript exists).

### p4-2.2.2 | 2.2.2 | Pause, Stop, Hide | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 3.A)
- Web: Verify auto-playing carousels, sliders, background videos, or auto-updating tickers lasting >5 seconds have a Pause/Stop control.
- PDF: Animated GIFs/multimedia in PDFs have pause/stop controls.
- Hint: Also check prefers-reduced-motion media query support as a best practice.

### p4-2.3.1 | 2.3.1 | Three Flashes or Below Threshold | A
- Section 508: E205.4
- Web: Verify no video, GIF, or UI animation flashes more than 3 times in any 1-second period.
- PDF: No flashing annotations or embedded media >3 Hz.

### p4-3.1.2 | 3.1.2 | Language of Parts | AA
- Section 508: E205.4
- Web: Listen with VoiceOver/NVDA to foreign-language phrases/quotes; verify lang attribute is set so screen reader switches pronunciation cleanly.
- PDF: Verify Tag Properties > Language is set on <Span> or <P> for foreign-language passages.

### p4-3.3.1 | 3.3.1 | Error Identification | A
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 5.D)
- Web: Submit forms with missing/invalid fields; verify errors are described in text (not color alone) and announced by VoiceOver/NVDA via aria-invalid and aria-describedby.
- PDF: Form validation alerts describe errors clearly in text.
- Hint: Best practice: display an error summary list at the top of the form with anchor links to invalid fields.

### p4-3.3.3 | 3.3.3 | Error Suggestion | AA
- Section 508: E205.4 (TT 5.D)
- Web: Trigger format/validation errors (e.g., bad email or date); verify error message suggests how to fix it and announces to screen readers.
- PDF: Form validation messages explain expected format.

### p4-3.3.4 | 3.3.4 | Error Prevention (Legal, Financial, Data) | AA
- Section 508: E205.4
- Web: On legal, financial, or data-deletion submissions, verify user can reverse submission, check/correct errors, or confirm before finalizing.
- PDF: Submittable forms allow review/confirmation before final submission.

### p4-4.1.2 | 4.1.2 | Name, Role, Value (Dynamic States & Screen Reader) | A

- PDF: Read PDF with VoiceOver/NVDA: verify all tags, alt text, table headers, and form states read in logical order.

### p4-4.1.3 | 4.1.3 | Status Messages | AA

- Web: Trigger toast notifications, search result counts, or loading spinners; verify VoiceOver/NVDA announces them via role='status' or aria-live without moving focus.
- PDF: N/A.
